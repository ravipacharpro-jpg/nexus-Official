# Weekly Review & Planning Skill

## When to use
- End of week review
- Planning next week
- Sprint retrospectives

## Process

### 1. Gather data
- Completed tasks this week
- In-progress tasks
- Blockers and issues
- Metrics (velocity, bugs, incidents)

### 2. Summarize
- Wins and accomplishments
- What didn't go well
- Surprises or learnings

### 3. Plan next week
- Top 3-5 priorities
- Dependencies and risks
- Capacity constraints

## Output format
```markdown
## Week of {Date}

### Completed
- ...

### In Progress
- ...

### Blockers
- ...

### Metrics
- ...

### Next Week Plan
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

### Risks
- ...
```
